## Hair Restoration with Dr. Katona

Dr. Katona offers the **Follicular Unit Excision (FUE)** hair transplant as the preferred solution. Unlike FUT (strip method), FUE is minimally invasive, leaves **no linear scar**, and avoids stitches and prolonged pain.

The goal is to help you regain your identity. Dr. Katona’s team assists in determining the most suitable procedure for optimal results. A fuller head of hair helps you look and feel younger, healthier, and more attractive, boosting self‑confidence.

### What is Hair Restoration?

Hair restoration slows or stops hair loss and restores hair to thinning or balding areas. Healthy follicles are extracted from the donor area (where hair is genetically resistant to DHT) and transplanted to balding areas for long‑term growth.

---

## Surgical Hair Transplants

Two main techniques: **FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation)** and **FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)**.

| | FUT | FUE |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction | A strip of scalp is removed, follicles dissected under microscope | Individual follicular units extracted directly |
| Scarring | Linear scar on back of head | Tiny dot‑like micro‑scars (virtually invisible) |
| Recovery | Longer, more post‑op discomfort | Faster healing, less swelling |
| Best for | Patients who don’t wear hair very short, need maximum grafts | Those who want to avoid linear scar, wear short hair, or have specific hair characteristics |

Both methods focus on extracting **follicular units** (1–4 hairs), the natural hair groupings. Success depends on graft survival, implantation angles, and the surgeon’s skill.

Dr. Katona’s surgical team is the first to be certified in all FUE procedures. Their clinic is the **only one offering FUE/FUT hybrid surgery** when needed.

## Beard Restoration

A beard transplant (facial hair transplant) uses FUE to move hair from the scalp (or other donor site) to the beard area to create a fuller, thicker beard.

**Procedure overview:**
1. **Consultation** to examine beard area and discuss goals.
2. **Harvesting** individual follicles from donor area (usually back of scalp).
3. **Graft preparation** into single‑unit follicular grafts.
4. **Transplantation** into tiny incisions at precise angles and density.
5. **Aftercare** instructions for optimal healing.

New beard growth typically appears within 3 months.

## Understanding Hair Loss

**Androgenetic Alopecia** (male/female pattern baldness) is the most common cause, responsible for over 95% of hair loss cases seeking treatment. Hair follicles on top of the scalp are genetically sensitive to DHT; donor hair from the back and sides is resistant and remains permanent after transplantation.

### Common Causes
- Genetics (male/female pattern baldness)
- Hormonal imbalances
- Thyroid disease
- Stress (telogen effluvium)
- Nutritional deficiencies
- Medications
- Traction alopecia (tight hairstyles)
- Autoimmune diseases (alopecia areata)
- Scarring alopecia

---

## Male Hair Loss

Male pattern baldness is classified by the **Norwood Scale** (7 stages). Treatment options include FDA‑approved medications (finasteride, minoxidil), low‑level laser therapy, and surgical hair restoration.

Other types:
- Telogen effluvium
- Anagen effluvium (chemotherapy)
- Alopecia areata
- Congenital triangular alopecia

---

## Female Hair Loss

Female pattern hair loss is often diffuse thinning (Ludwig Scale). Psychological effects can be severe. Treatment options include topical minoxidil, hair transplantation (FUT or FUE), and cosmetic camouflaging agents.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**What is a hair transplant?**  
Surgical relocation of hair follicles from donor area to balding/thinning areas.

**Who is a good candidate?**  
Those with sufficient donor hair and realistic expectations. Proper medical management of ongoing hair loss is recommended.

**How does FUE work?**  
Individual follicular units are extracted with micro‑punches and transplanted, leaving no linear scar.

**Is it painful?**  
Local anaesthesia makes the procedure virtually pain‑free. Mild post‑op discomfort is easily managed.

**Are results permanent?**  
Transplanted hair is permanent; however, progressive loss in non‑transplanted areas may require future sessions.

**Risks?**  
Minor swelling, shock loss (temporary), rare infection. Choosing a qualified, board‑certified surgeon minimises risks.

**Cost?**  
From $4,000 to $16,000+ depending on graft count.

**Do you perform other cosmetic procedures?**  
Dr. Katona specialises exclusively in hair restoration.

**Does FUE work for African‑American patients?**  
Yes, with great success.
